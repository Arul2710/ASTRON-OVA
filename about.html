About Astronova

Our Mission Is Simple:
Make the Universe Accessible.

We believe the wonder of the cosmos belongs to everyone — not just scientists in observatories. We bring it directly to schools, communities and doorsteps.

Our Story

Where Wonder Begins

Astronova began with a single telescope and a passionate group of astronomy educators who saw the spark in students' eyes when they looked at the night sky. Today we bring that wonder to thousands through mobile planetariums and hands-on programs.

10+Years Sharing Space
50KStudents Reached

Our Mission

To deliver immersive astronomy education that turns curiosity into discovery — making high-quality cosmic experiences accessible to every school, family and community, regardless of location.

Our Vision

A world where every child has looked up at the stars, asked a big question, and felt inspired to explore the universe. We envision the next generation of scientists, dreamers and explorers.
