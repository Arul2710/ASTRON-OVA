Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our resources, programs and getting started with Astronova.

What resources do you offer for educators?

Ready-to-print posters, classroom timelines, lesson plans and STEM activity guides that align with TEKS and NGSS standards. Download them free from this Resources page.

Are the downloadable resources free?

Yes. All educator resources are free to download and print for classroom use. Just browse the catalog above and click "Download Posters".

Can you bring a planetarium dome to our school?

Absolutely. Our mobile planetarium visits schools across the region. Visit the Programs page to explore assembly and STEM workshop options for your grade level.

How do I book a telescope night or stargazing session?

Head to the Contact page or use the "Book Now" buttons on the Programs page. Our team will match a session to your audience, date and location.

How can I stay updated on upcoming celestial events?

Check the Celestial Calendar above regularly, or contact us to join our mailing list for monthly sky alerts and observing tips.
